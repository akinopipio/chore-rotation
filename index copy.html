Chore rotation

Everyone shifts one chore each week. Tick a chore when it's done.

House rules for everyone

  • Wipe down the counters and stove after you cook or eat.
  • Teaming up is fine. Ask someone to help with your chore, as long as it gets done.
  • Forgetting the everyday stuff, like wiping the counters, is okay. No one will be mad. Just do it next time.
  • Your assigned chore is different. It needs to get done that week.

Trip rules

  • Tell the house at least 2–3 days before you leave, and tap “Mark away” on your card for each week you're gone. Your teammate takes over automatically.
  • Short trip (up to 3 days): do your chore before you leave or when you're back. It still needs to get done that week.
  • Medium trip (4 to 7 days): swap with someone before you leave, or ask someone to cover it and return the favor next time.
  • Long trip (over a week): the others cover your chores while you're away, and you pick up a bit extra when you're back.
  • A chore missed because of a trip you announced in time never counts against you.
  • We work in teams: Sofi + Vale, and Nico + Ya Ching. When one of you is away, your teammate does your chores that week. Return the favor when they're away.
  • If you and your teammate are both away, ask someone from the other team to cover. Assign them on the chore. You'll owe them a favor, and you pay it back later by taking one of their chores (“pays back” button).

Strike rules

  • A strike means your assigned chore wasn't done by the end of the week and wasn't covered or excused by a trip you announced in time.
  • Strike 1: a warning, and you do the chore within 24 hours.
  • Strike 2: you do your own chore plus one of someone else's chores next week.
  • Strike 3: you do every chore in the house for one week by yourself.
